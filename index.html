Traveler's Guide & Local Info

Getting Here

By Air: Nearest airport is Butuan City Airport (45 mins away)

By Land: 6-7 hours from Manila or 1.5 hours from Surigao City

From Butuan City: Van/Bus 30-45 mins (PHP 50-70)

Local Transportation

🚌 Ceres Bus: Regular service to/from Butuan
🛺 Tricycles: PHP 10-50 per trip (main transportation)
🏍️ Motorcycle Rentals: PHP 300-500/day
🚗 Car Rentals: Contact Tourism Office (085) 817-1001

Weather & Best Time to Visit

Best Season: December to May (Dry Season)

Avg Temperature: 25-32°C year-round

Rainy Season: June to November (occasional flooding)

Typhoon Risk: Minimal in Caraga region

Local Cuisine & Dining

🍲 Sinuglaw - grilled pork + fish ceviche (specialty)
🌾 Palagsing - local sticky rice dessert
🍤 Fresh Seafood - daily catch from local fishermen
🥥 Buko Juice - fresh coconut from nearby farms

Major Events & Festivals

Kahimunan Festival (May) - Celebrates local culture & harvest
Local fiestas - Various barangay celebrations throughout the year
Holy Week - Religious processions & events

Popular Activities

Mountain Climbing: Mag-Inde Peak, lush rainforests
Swimming: Natural pools, waterfalls (Agusan River)
Camping: Designated campsites for adventure seekers
Hiking: Nature trails with scenic views

Accommodation Tips

Hotels: Budget to mid-range options available (PHP 800-3,000/night)
Booking: Reserve in advance during peak season (Dec-Jan)
Facilities: Most places have A/C, WiFi, & hot water

Money & Budget Guide

Currency: Philippine Peso (PHP) - 1 USD ≈ 56 PHP
Daily Budget: PHP 1,500-2,500 for comfortable travel
ATMs: Available in city proper & nearby bancarios

Health & Safety

Medical: Hospital available in city proper (085) 817-3333
Pharmacy: Several pharmacies with basic medications
Travel Tips: Bring insect repellent & sunscreen
